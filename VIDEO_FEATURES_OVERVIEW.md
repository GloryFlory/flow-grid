## Notes for Designer

- **Show real interactions** - Clicking, dragging, typing (not just static screens)
- **Mobile is key** - Give mobile views prominent placement
- **Keep it moving** - Fast enough to stay engaging, slow enough to understand
- **Professional but warm** - Not corporate/stuffy, not too casual/playful
- **End with confidence** - Clear call-to-action, inspiring tagline

---

**The Big Idea**: We're not just listing features. We're showing the transformation from "event planning is stressful" to "I'm proud to share this schedule with my community."
