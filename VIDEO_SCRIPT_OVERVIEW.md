## Presentation Principles

### 1. Show, Don't Tell
❌ "Flow Grid has a booking system"
✅ Show someone clicking "Book Session" → capacity updates → "5 spots left"

### 2. Real-World Context
❌ Generic "Session 1, Session 2, Session 3"
✅ "Morning Vinyasa Flow with Sarah," "Ecstatic Dance & Cacao Ceremony"

### 3. Mobile-First Always
Every feature demo should show mobile responsiveness or mobile-first design

### 4. Emotional Beats
- **Problem**: Frustration, overwhelm
- **First Look**: Relief, curiosity
- **Setup**: Confidence, ease
- **Public Schedule**: Pride, excitement
- **Admin Tools**: Control, professionalism
- **Closing**: Inspiration, readiness

### 5. Pacing
- Fast enough to maintain energy
- Slow enough to comprehend each feature
- Let beautiful visuals breathe (don't rush past the public schedule)

## Voiceover Tone Guide

### Style
- **Warm, not corporate** - Friend explaining, not sales pitch
- **Confident, not arrogant** - We know we solve this well
- **Clear, not technical** - Avoid jargon, use simple language

### Pacing
- **Moderate speed** - Not rushed, not dragging
- **Strategic pauses** - After key claims, before transitions
- **Emphasis words**: "under 10 minutes," "mobile-first," "completely free," "beautiful"

### Avoid
- ❌ Corporate speak ("leverage," "synergize," "ecosystem")
- ❌ Superlatives without proof ("best," "revolutionary," "game-changing")
- ❌ Technical jargon ("API," "webhook," "responsive grid")
- ❌ Apologetic language ("just," "simply," "only")

### Embrace
- ✅ Direct benefits ("create in minutes," "works on any device")
- ✅ Specific numbers ("10 minutes," "5 free events")
- ✅ Relatable scenarios ("your attendees," "your team")
- ✅ Confident claims we can prove ("beautiful," "professional," "easy")
